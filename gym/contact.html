Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before stepping onto the training floor.

What do I need to bring for my 1-Day VIP Pass? +
Just bring valid government photo identification, clean indoor workout footwear, and your training gear. We provide complimentary luxury towel service, locker locks, and premium shower amenities including Malin+Goetz body care.
How does the 24/7 keyless biometric access work? +
Upon joining, our team registers your smartphone NFC digital pass and optional facial biometric scan at the front desk. This enables hands-free instant entry into the facility at any hour of the day or night, 365 days a year.
Can your dietitians build custom diet plans for food allergies? +
Yes! Our in-house registered dietitians formulate hyper-personalized protocols tailored to gluten sensitivities, lactose intolerance, FODMAP limits, vegan requirements, and athletic competition prep timelines.
Are there contracts or cancellation penalties? +
None whatsoever. We believe in earning your loyalty every single workout. Monthly memberships can be paused or canceled anytime with a simple 30-day email notification to concierge@apextitan.com.
